THE STORY OF HOMEMADED

Why a Cloud Kitchen? Because flavor deserves full focus.

Most restaurants spend 70% of their energy on decor, valet parking, and crowded dining rooms. We stripped away the noise to invest 100% into ingredients, heritage techniques, and pure homestyle taste.

Stone-Ground Batter

Real batter takes time. Whole urad dal and Ponni rice are slowly ground on traditional granite stones and fermented naturally overnight without baking soda. Soft, light, and easy on your stomach.

Clay Pot Simmer

Our sambars and curries simmer unhurried in seasoned earthenware over gentle flames. Spices release their rich oils naturally, without chemical tastemakers or artificial food coloring.

Pure Ghee & Cold-Pressed Oils

We cook strictly with cold-pressed gingelly oil, fresh coconut oil, and golden desi ghee. Zero palm oil, zero adulteration—clean, honest homestyle food you can comfortably enjoy every day.

Fresh Banana Leaf Wrap

Straight from our cast-iron tawa, your hot food is carefully nestled inside fresh banana leaves before boxing. Natural warmth and aroma stay locked in with zero plastic touching your meal.
